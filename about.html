Company Profile

A Heritage of Trust, Innovation & Refreshing Flavours

Espresso Tech Vending Solution (under our flagship brands ALINZA and CAFE RICH) has carved a distinctive leadership position in manufacturing electronic and beverage-vending machines, tea & coffee premixes, and specialty heating systems.

Our range of beverage-vending machines, digital boilers, instant tea premixes, coffee blends, and soup mixes is manufactured using premium grade raw materials procured from verified global suppliers. Before being supplied to our clients, every machine and batch of premix is rigorously tested in our in-house quality assurance laboratory in Ghatkopar, Mumbai, ensuring flawless reliability and supply across PAN India.
